Final Presentation:

Remember that there are a lot of people to present so to be respectful to all participants, please keep your presentation short

Title

  • Add the name of your project and names of Team members

Slide 1: About Me/Us

  • Add brief information about your country, your job title, your workplace(s), and areas of interest/focus

Slide 2: Background

  • Add VERY SHORT introduction to your disease/health issue. This should be 1-3 sentences or bullets

    • Name of Disease

    • Current Status of health condition/issue/disease

    • Who is affected

    • How their lives are effected

  • Add VERY SHORT reason why this disease/health issue is important to your country. This should be 1-2 sentences or bullets

Slide 3: Research Question

  • Describe the decision problem you are trying to solve in one sentence using the PICOT formatSlide 4: Research Question

Slide 4: Strategy Options

  • Describe each strategy being modeled in one sentence

  • Graphically display differences between the strategies
